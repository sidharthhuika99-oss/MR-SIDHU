PS POWER SERVICE WEBSITE
========================
Open index.html in a browser.

Included:
- Responsive homepage
- Uploaded PS Power Service logo in header/favicon/footer
- Electrical service cards
- Lenter / Slab Piping service
- Direct service booking form
- Customer register/login demo
- My Bookings and booking status
- Call + WhatsApp buttons
- Gallery, reviews and contact sections

IMPORTANT:
This version is a front-end demo. Customer accounts and bookings are stored in the browser's localStorage, so it is NOT suitable for production authentication or real database storage.
For a live production website, connect the booking/authentication system to a secure backend/database and replace the placeholder phone/email/address with the real business details.
